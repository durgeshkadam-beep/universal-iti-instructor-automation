# Universal EduSkill 360 — Innovation Screening Brief (2026)

**Status:** Working prototype and isolated staging pilot under validation; not a production-wide deployment, certified AI product, measured employment-impact study or placement service.

**Concept and development:** Durgesh Dnyandev Kadam, Craft Instructor (DTP / DTPO), Government ITI Mumbai-01.

**Purpose:** Help vocational trainees turn daily learning into **instructor-verified skill evidence**, identify specific learning gaps and prepare for further training or career exploration. Give instructors a simpler, connected way to plan, teach, verify, assess and maintain records.

> **In one sentence:** Universal EduSkill 360 connects an instructor's training workflow to a trainee's verified Skill Passport, focused improvement plan and evidence-linked career preparation, without making unverified job or AI claims.

This brief follows the Maharashtra Zone Seva First Innovation Challenge's six-part presentation structure; it is also readable as plain text without logging in, JavaScript, a GitHub account or an AI model.

## 1. Innovation and applicant snapshot

- **Project:** Universal EduSkill 360
- **Sector focus:** Vocational training, education delivery and youth skilling
- **Project stage:** Working software prototype, isolated Firebase staging and iterative automated validation
- **Pilot context:** DTP / DTPO training workflow at Government ITI Mumbai-01; one instructor normally manages one batch of up to 24 trainees. This is a *planned operating unit*, not a claim that 24 trainees have completed the pilot.
- **Primary beneficiaries:** Trainees and craft instructors
- **Supporting users:** Principal, admin and institute staff, with distinct permission-scoped portals

## 2. Problem — what needs solving?

Training calendars, practical completion, attendance, learning assessments and employment preparation often live in disconnected forms. Repetitive instructor documentation consumes attention; trainees may have little clarity about **which practical skills an instructor has actually verified**, what still needs work and how their learning relates to career options. Simply displaying vacancies or an AI chatbot does not close this gap.

## 3. Solution and distinct approach

**Instructor:** Plan the training year and day-wise syllabus; manage Theory and Practicals; record attendance and assessments; check verified practical evidence; prepare and review lesson or demonstration plans; export/print authorised records.

**Trainee:** Review their own learning/attendance, distinguish self-ratings from *instructor-verified* achievements, see practical-based Skill Gap and next learning actions, practise resumes/interviews in Marathi, Hindi or English and explore reviewed career information without fabricated employment data.

**Connected journey:**

1. Instructor teaches a practical.
2. Instructor records evidence and verifies the skill.
3. The trainee sees verified evidence in My Skills / Skill Passport.
4. Skill Gap identifies missing skills relative to a trainee-selected target.
5. The trainee receives a focused learning path and can inspect source-linked career examples.

**What is distinctive:** The value is the *traceable link* from normal institute teaching to verified individual progress and next actions—not a claim to have invented attendance software, generative AI or job portals.

## 4. Demonstrated feasibility and implementation

- Responsive web application with role-based screens for Student, Instructor, Principal, Admin and Staff.
- Firebase authentication and permission-controlled Firestore collections; scoped read/write rules and synthetic emulator security tests.
- Isolated staging project; no production student-data migration implied.
- Optional secured backend services for features that need server verification, such as exam grading and optional generative AI.
- Tests include unit, web build, container, Firestore/Auth emulator and desktop/mobile end-to-end checks. Individual revisions are judged only by their own successful CI; see [current validation results](https://github.com/durgeshkadam-beep/universal-iti-instructor-automation/actions/workflows/v15-production-tests.yml), not a historical green check.
- Development progress is auditable in [GitHub PR #16](https://github.com/durgeshkadam-beep/universal-iti-instructor-automation/pull/16) and [source repository](https://github.com/durgeshkadam-beep/universal-iti-instructor-automation).
- [Staging demonstration](https://universal-iti-dk-staging.web.app/v15/) is a **test system**; some features require an approved synthetic account. Site contents can lag behind the latest branch until a verified hosting release is performed.

**AI honesty:** Keyless structured learning/career practice is not generated by a language model. Instructor/student generative conversations and AI-made drafts depend on an enabled, authorised backend provider, consent and quota. Do **not** treat a visible AI button, passing emulator test or free-tier provider option as proof that live generation is currently available to every user.

## 5. Expected impact, cost and scalability

**Potential gains to validate, not established outcomes:**
- Fewer repeated record-entry steps for the instructor.
- Better visibility of strengths and practical gaps for individual trainees.
- A clear follow-up action after every verified practical rather than generic employment advice.
- Better institute-level traceability with role-based oversight.

**Cost model:** Browser access and keyless guided practice avoid mandatory per-chat charges. Actual recurring expense for hosting, security, Firebase usage, backups, model quotas and support remains to be measured during a pilot. No claim of a fully zero-cost institution-wide rollout.

**Scale path:** Start with one authorised DTP/DTPO batch; collect consented feedback and measured completion times; verify value and accessibility; then configure additional trades, instructors and institutes using the same permission-scoped workspace model. No verified statewide adoption, placements, salary uplift, 20% time savings or youth reach figure is claimed.
