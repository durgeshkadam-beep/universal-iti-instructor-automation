# V12 Free AI + Calendar Automation

## Free workflow
1. Instructor logs in.
2. Upload syllabus.
3. Use Gemini API key for one-click AI, or Manual AI mode with any free chatbot.
4. Review theory/practical classification and hours.
5. Load Maharashtra 2026 holidays, add institute/local/future official dates, and generate the working calendar.
6. Create Complete Training Plan.
7. Today's Teaching uses the saved day-wise plan.
8. Generate Lesson/Demonstration Plan, review it, then Approve & Keep.
9. Mark Conducted after teaching.

## Calendar rules
- Monday-Saturday may be selected as working days.
- Sundays/non-selected weekdays are non-working.
- 2nd and 4th Saturday can be excluded automatically.
- Maharashtra 2026 government holiday preset is included.
- Existing institute holidays can be merged.
- 2027/future holidays must be added when officially published.
- Theory/practical work is allocated by hours, not by assuming two full training days on the same date.

## Data/privacy
- Gemini key is session-only in browser sessionStorage.
- AI output is always a draft until instructor approval.
- Do not send trainee personal identifiers to free AI services.
