## Core problem
Many learners do not see the industry value of course skills or their next career learning action while still enrolled. Instructors use time on fragmented attendance, planning, assessments and reports instead of individual mentoring. This project addresses both together.

## Proposed solution
Learner practicals -> instructor-reviewed evidence -> verified Skill Passport -> skill gaps -> reviewed career sources -> next action, complemented by instructor teaching-workflow automation. The first ITI working prototype covers major components, with an integrated occupation/use-case Lens remaining a planned product extension.

## Innovation
The two-way learner + educator workflow is the differentiator, rather than ordinary attendance digitisation. A free-first design provides Marathi/Hindi/English structured guidance without an AI key; generative model use is optional and currently not enabled.

## Potential beneficiaries, not measured impact
MSDE/PIB reports 1,021 Maharashtra ITIs (2026) and 1,30,810 enrolments (2025–26), demonstrating possible sector reach—not prevalence of the problem, app users, jobs or achieved impact.

## Proposed pilot, not existing results
One approved ITI, about 40 trainee volunteers in two batches, two educators, 90 days. Measure career awareness, plan completion, time spent on two administrative tasks, instructor-verified practicals and privacy/usability. Stretch targets: 70% completing a career plan and 20% time reduction. Indicative budget INR 20,000; quotes, permissions and outcomes pending.

## Expansion
Later trade-by-trade and institutional pilots, then adapted curricula, privacy and child-safety reviews for schools, colleges and universities. No production rollout in these is claimed.
